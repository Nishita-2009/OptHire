# OptHire Hiring Fairness & AI Ethics Principles

OptHire is designed as an intelligent **decision-support platform**, not an autonomous hiring authority. All evaluations, match scores, gap analyses, and recommendations are designed to assist human recruiters and hiring managers in making informed, objective, and unbiased hiring decisions.

---

## 1. Non-Autonomous Decision Support
- **Human in the Loop**: OptHire outputs provide candidate synthesis, structured insights, and preliminary scoring. Final hiring, interview progression, and offer decisions require human review.
- **Explicit Disclaimers**: All generated reports contain the mandatory notice:
  > *"This AI-generated report is decision support only. Final hiring decisions should include appropriate human review."*

---

## 2. Protection Against Bias & Irrelevant Characteristics
- **Protected Personal Characteristics**: Evaluation prompts strictly prohibit considering demographic or personal characteristics—including age, gender, race, ethnicity, religion, marital status, sexual orientation, disability, or nationality.
- **Evidence-Based Evaluation**: Scoring must derive strictly from demonstrated technical competencies, verified work experience, educational background, and substantiated interview responses.

---

## 3. Explainable Scoring
- **No Black-Box Scores**: Every score (Overall Match, Skill Match, Experience Match, Education Match, and Interview Performance) includes an explicit explanatory rationale (`reason`, `strengths`, and `missing_points`).
- **Transparency**: Recruiter notes and candidate summaries surface clear, traceable justifications for every recommendation.

---

## 4. Nuanced Handling of Missing Information
- **Neutral Interpretation**: Missing data (such as omitted optional certifications, unlisted hobbies, or unstated graduation years) is not automatically penalized as negative evidence.
- **Confidence Calibration**: When key data points are absent or an interview session is partial, the platform down-weights `decision_confidence` rather than lowering the candidate's core competency score.

---

## 5. Rubric-Based Interview Evaluation
- **Standardized Question Rubrics**: Each interview question generated by Agent 5 includes pre-defined `purpose`, `expected_answer_points`, and `evaluation_criteria`.
- **Consistent Scoring Bands**:
  - `90 – 100`: Excellent
  - `75 – 89`: Strong
  - `60 – 74`: Acceptable
  - `40 – 59`: Weak
  - `0 – 39`: Poor
- **Evidence-Only Assessment**: Evaluators score only what the candidate stated without speculating or hallucinating unmentioned qualifications.
